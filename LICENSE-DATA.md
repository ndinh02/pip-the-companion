## Covered
- `data/` — generated matches, 5 Hz tracking, player statistics, demo match, content-workflow traces.
- `web/public/data/` — the match files the app plays.
- `match-reader/templates/` — the "Call it!" question and explanation texts (EN, DE, VI).
- `schemas/` — the data formats (also usable under MIT, like the code).

Everything in these files is synthetic: the teams (Harbour Lights, Copperfield), players, events, positions and numbers
are generated by code in this repository. They do not describe any real match, club or person.
